Clearance

What happens when a container lands

Documents checked

Original or telex-released B/L, invoice, packing list and certificates checked before arrival.

Goods declaration

Filed through our licensed customs agent on Pakistan Customs' WeBOC / PSW system under PCT 6309.

Examination

Customs examination where required, then duties and taxes paid on the assessed value.

Release & transport

Container released within the free days and trucked to our Karachi warehouse.

Inspection report

Sample bales from the front, middle and back opened, weighed and photographed.

Report to supplier

Grade and weight report within 14 days, with evidence for any claim.

Documents

What we need with each shipment

  • Commercial invoice
  • Bale-wise packing list
  • Bill of lading
  • Certificate of origin
  • Fumigation / disinfection certificate
  • Insurance certificate
  • Pre-shipment inspection certificate (first orders)

Container standard

Mainly 40ft High Cube full container loads (FCL); 20ft depending on the goods. Typical net load 22–26 tons of bales, loaded tight from floor to roof.

Loading video

A short video of the loading and the sealed doors, with the seal number visible, for every container.

Independent inspection

For first orders we appoint SGS, Bureau Veritas or Intertek for pre-shipment inspection, at our cost.
