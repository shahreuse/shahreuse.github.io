Why Italy first

Winter stock, organised collection, direct sailings

Italy has a large, organised network of used-clothing collection and sorting companies, and its collections are rich in the jackets, coats and knitwear that make up most Pakistani demand.

Containers from Genoa and La Spezia reach Karachi in roughly 3–4 weeks, depending on the carrier and routing, much faster than our North American lanes.

Who we want to hear from

  • Registered graders and sorting plants with export experience
  • Collectors able to load original (unsorted) 40ft containers
  • Companies clearing warehouse, surplus or end-of-season stock
  • Exporters already shipping to Pakistan, Asia or Africa
The process

From first offer to regular containers

Specification & offer

We share our category mix and grades; you quote per kg FOB and CFR Karachi / Port Qasim.

Verification

Exchange of company registrations, a video call of your sorting floor and trade references.

Contract & payment security

Proforma invoice with grades, bale weights and a claims clause. Payment by letter of credit or documents through our bank.

Inspection & loading

Pre-shipment inspection (SGS, Bureau Veritas or Intertek) for first orders, plus a loading video of every container.

Clearance in Karachi

Filed through our licensed customs agent under PCT 6309, released within the line's free days.

Feedback & repeat

Grade and weight report within 14 days of arrival, then the next order on a regular schedule.
