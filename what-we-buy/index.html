Our standards

What every container must meet

These rules protect you and us at Karachi customs, and keep our buyers coming back.

  • Clean, dry, reusable goods with a fumigation or disinfection certificate
  • Contents exactly as per the agreed list, grades and bale weights
  • No textile waste, rags for recycling, wet or mouldy goods
  • No appliances, electronics, new goods or any prohibited items
  • Unsaleable content within the limit agreed in the contract
