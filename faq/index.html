Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

For exporters supplying us and for wholesale buyers in Pakistan.

For suppliers

How many containers do you import?

Our team imports 15–20 40ft containers a month, today from the USA and Canada. We are adding European suppliers, starting with Italy.

Which grades do you buy?

Every grade: Cream, A, B, C, mixed and original (unsorted). We work to your written grade definitions.

Do you buy 20ft containers?

Yes, depending on the goods. Most of our orders are full 40ft High Cube loads of 22–26 tons, but we also buy 20ft containers, for example for shoes, accessories, Cream grade or trial lots.

What payment terms do you offer?

Letter of credit at sight or documents against payment through our business bank account. We never pay in cash or through third parties.

Which port do you ship to?

Karachi Port or Port Qasim, Pakistan. Please quote CFR Karachi as well as FOB.

What documents do you need?

Commercial invoice, bale-wise packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, fumigation or disinfection certificate, insurance certificate and, for first orders, a pre-shipment inspection certificate.

How do you handle claims?

We inspect sample bales from the front, middle and back of every container and send a grade and weight report within 14 days. Claims are raised only with photo and video evidence, within the agreed period.

Do you buy warehouse clearance or surplus stock?

Yes. Clearance, surplus and end-of-season lots are welcome, as long as the goods are clean, reusable and fully listed.

For wholesale buyers

Where is your warehouse?

Sher Shah, Karachi 75730. Contact us on WhatsApp before visiting so that the right stock is ready to open.

What is the minimum order?

You can buy from a single bale up to a full container load. Mixed-category orders are welcome.

Do you deliver outside Karachi?

Yes. We load your transporter or arrange goods transport to your city at cost.

How do I get your rates?

Message us on WhatsApp for this season’s wholesale rate list. Rates change with each container and season.

Still have a question?

Email or WhatsApp us. We reply within two working days.
