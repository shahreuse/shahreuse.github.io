Who we are

A Karachi import house built on the used-clothing trade

Shah Reuse & Trading Enterprise is a partner-owned firm of importers and local wholesalers in Karachi, the centre of Pakistan's wholesale market for used clothing. Our team brings in 15–20 40ft containers every month and sells them on to wholesalers, sub-wholesalers, retailers and online resellers across the country.

We know what every grade is worth in every Pakistani market, which is why we can buy any category and any grade and still pay on time, container after container.

Containers land at Karachi Port (KICT, PICT, SAPT) or Port Qasim (QICT) and are cleared under PCT 6309 through our licensed customs agent, with every document in order.

How we work with suppliers

Simple, documented and bank-secured

Specification & offer

We share our category mix and grades; you quote per kg FOB and CFR Karachi / Port Qasim.

Verification

Exchange of company registrations, a video call of your sorting floor and trade references.

Contract & payment security

Proforma invoice with grades, bale weights and a claims clause. Payment by letter of credit or documents through our bank.

Inspection & loading

Pre-shipment inspection (SGS, Bureau Veritas or Intertek) for first orders, plus a loading video of every container.

Clearance in Karachi

Filed through our licensed customs agent under PCT 6309, released within the line's free days.

Feedback & repeat

Grade and weight report within 14 days of arrival, then the next order on a regular schedule.

Bank-secured payment

Letter of credit at sight or documents against payment, through a scheduled bank in Pakistan. You ship knowing the money is secured.

Fraud protection

Bank details are agreed once, in writing, on the proforma invoice and are never changed by email. Always confirm any request by phone.

Verify us first

Video call with our partners, a visit to our Karachi warehouse and trade references, all before the first order.

Compliant imports

Declared under PCT 6309 at true value and weight, with fumigation certificates. Reuse goods only, in line with EU Regulation 2024/1157.

Independent inspection

Pre-shipment inspection by SGS, Bureau Veritas or Intertek for first orders, at our cost, plus a loading video for every container.

Fair, written claims

A claims clause agreed up front, evidence-based reports, and your prices kept confidential. An NDA is available on request.
