About SRTE

Importers and wholesalers who know the used-clothing trade from the ground up

A partner-owned business in Karachi, the heart of Pakistan’s used-clothing trade.

Our story

Built in the market we sell to

Shah Reuse & Trading Enterprise (SRTE) brings together partners with hands-on experience of the used-clothing trade: buying containers, clearing them in Karachi and selling bale by bale to the wholesalers who supply Pakistan's markets.

Our team imports 15–20 40ft containers every month, today sourced from graders and collectors in the USA and Canada. We buy sorted clothing, footwear and household textiles in every grade, plus original (unsorted) loads that we sort ourselves in Karachi.

We are now expanding into Europe, beginning with Italy, to add the winter-rich, consistently graded stock that Pakistani buyers ask for most.

“We would rather work with three reliable partners for ten years than with thirty for one season.”

Shah Faisal, Chief Executive Officer

Our values

What partners can expect from us

Secure payments

Payments only through our business bank account, by L/C or documents. Never cash or third parties.

Accurate declarations

Worn clothing declared under PCT 6309 at true value and weight.

Fast release

Prompt document handling and container release, so no demurrage is left with your shipping line.

Fair claims

Claims raised only with photo and video evidence, within the agreed period. Your prices stay confidential.

Reuse, not waste

Second-hand clothing is reuse

Every garment we sell is worn again in Pakistan. We buy only sorted clothing for reuse, or original collections that we sort ourselves, never textile waste.

  • Sorted for reuse, in line with the EU Waste Shipment Regulation (EU) 2024/1157 for European exports
  • Clean, dry goods with a fumigation or disinfection certificate
  • Nothing outside the agreed list in the container: no appliances and no waste
  • Company registration (NTN, sales tax and Pakistan Single Window) and our bank reference supplied with every purchase order
